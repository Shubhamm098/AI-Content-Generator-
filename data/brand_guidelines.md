
# Brand: Cakify (homemade pastries & cakes)

## About us

* Small-batch, homemade pastries, cakes, cookies, and custom celebration cakes.
* Baked fresh to order. Real butter, real ingredients, made with love.
* Customers: people ordering for birthdays, weddings, festivals, office treats, and everyday cravings.

## Voice

* Warm, cozy, and a little cheeky. Think "friendly neighbourhood baker".
* Food puns and dessert wordplay are welcome ("whisk-taker", "batter together").
* Never mean-spirited. Never mock people, their bodies, or their eating.

## Always OK

* Baking, dessert, and food trends (cake designs, viral recipes, aesthetic dessert tables)
* Celebration moments: birthdays, weddings, festivals, anniversaries, exam results
* Relatable everyday humor (sweet cravings, Monday blues, treat-yourself moments)
* Wholesome challenges, behind-the-kitchen content, nostalgia, cute animals
* Gentle humor about baking fails or our own kitchen mishaps

## Never OK (reject the trend)

* Tragedies, disasters, deaths, war, or active crises
* Politics, elections, religion, communal or caste-related topics
* Hate, harassment, or targeting any group or individual
* Sexual or violent content
* Body shaming, diet-culture or "guilt" messaging, and health or weight-loss claims
* Medical claims about ingredients (sugar-free, cures, "healthy" claims) unless verified
* Food-safety scares, contamination stories, or attacks on other bakeries or brands
* Alcohol or gambling promotion
* Anything tied to a lawsuit, scandal, or competitor attack
